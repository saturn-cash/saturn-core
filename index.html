Saturn is a coin you can actually mine.

It's a proof-of-work coin forked from Bitcoin Core. The chain is running, there's a public pool and a block explorer, and the desktop app makes your wallet and mines for you. No download? Mine and check your wallet right in your browser, even on a phone.

Saturn, the desktop app

One installer. It creates your wallets and backs them up with a 12-word recovery phrase, mines to the pool or solo, sends and receives SAT, and sets up Bitaxe miners on your network. Flip one switch and it runs a full Saturn node on your computer too.

Not at your computer? Mine in your browser on any phone, tablet or laptop, and load your wallet there to watch your balance and payouts. Nothing to install. The app is still the way to go for real speed, sending SAT and running a node; the browser is the quick way in.

It's a beta, so expect some rough edges. If something breaks, open an issue on GitHub and I'll look at it.

Already have a miner?

Anything that speaks SHA-256d over stratum can connect to the pool. Use your SAT address as the username.

# point cpuminer at the pool

cpuminer -a sha256d \
  -o stratum+tcp://pool.saturn.boats:3333 \
  -u YOUR_SAT_ADDRESS -p x

# or build and run your own node

git clone https://github.com/saturn-cash/saturn-core
cd saturn-core
cmake -B build -DENABLE_IPC=OFF && cmake --build build -j8
./build/bin/saturnd -daemon -addnode=pool.saturn.boats:9999
./build/bin/saturn-cli getblockchaininfo

Design the Saturn coin

This is the coin for now. Think you can do better? Send in your own Saturn coin or logo. We look at every one, and the best ones might end up on the site, the pool and the app.

1 SAT = 100,000,000 rings. A ring is the smallest piece of a SAT, like a satoshi for Bitcoin. Convert on the pool ↗

By sending a design you’re saying you made it and Saturn can use it. Only the Saturn team sees submissions.
