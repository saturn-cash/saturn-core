SAT

Payouts are PPLNS: each block's reward is split by the work every miner did over the last two blocks' worth of shares, so steady miners are paid fairly and hopping in and out doesn't pay. Rewards unlock after 101 blocks, then go to the sat1 address you mine with. Mining solo (password solo) skips the split: a block you find pays its whole reward straight to your address.

Bitaxe

The easiest way to mine real blocks at home.

  1. Open AxeOS or the Bitaxe app, then Settings → Pool.
  2. URL pool.saturn.boats, port 3333.
  3. User sat1…address.bitaxe, password x. Save and restart.

Saturn Miner

The desktop app for macOS, Windows and Linux. Mines with your CPU, shows your earnings and sets up a Bitaxe for you.

  1. Install it and open Mining.
  2. Paste your sat1… address and press the hexagon.
